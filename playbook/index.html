"What did you do next? Who did you go to?"

Ask

"Tell me about something that failed in that job. Why did it fail, or what was your part in it?"

"What did you do next? Who did you go to?"

What it tells you

Exit reason. Growth, money, distance, manager, "didn't like it". Note it as said.

What it tells you

Ownership vs blaming. Do they name their own part, or is it the market, the manager, the team, the leads?

Passive waiting vs seeking support. After the failure, did they go to someone and ask, or wait for things to change on their own? Short, specific answers are good. Long excuses are not.

Give feedback — if there is a communication gap

"Speak a little more confidently. Talk to me the way you would talk to a relative, not the way you read from a page."

Stage 2

Role clarity, conviction & need

ConvictionJob needRole clarity
Ask

"Would you have paid for an upskilling program in college, around ₹4,700 a month? Why?"

What it tells you

Conviction. A real reason from their own life means they can believe it for a student. A flat "no" or "don't know" is a flag.

Ask

"If you are not selected here, what will you do next?"

What it tells you

Job need. Does this job matter to them now, or is it one of many options?

Ask

"What is the hardest thing you've achieved? What did you give up for it?"

What it tells you

Drive. Listen for sacrifice and sustained effort over months, not a one-day event. Note it as evidence under Conviction + Need.

Ask

What do you think the BDA role is?

Then clarify

Calling students and parents to sell a paid course. Rejections will come daily.

Stage 3

Give instruction → Take mock → Give feedback → Retake → Repeat

Rapport buildingRejection handlingActive listening & understandingCoachability
Before you start

Ask "Did you listen to the reference call?" If not, reschedule.

Instruction 1 · you say

"You are the CGE, I am the parent. Talk to me as if you speak to your favourite uncle — then only rapport will be built. Start with the intro."

Check · rapport building

Speaking with feel & emotion vs reading

Objection · you say

"We don't need all this now."

Check · rejection handling

Staying calm vs panicking

Trying to make them realise vs giving up

Give feedback — if not properly told

"It's not just about joining a course. You want your child to settle well in their career, right? But many are jobless. Only if we know why they are jobless can we guide your child properly, right? That's the guidance we give here."

Retake the mock

Stop mock 1 here.

Instruction 2 · you say

"Everything is fine for me. Now tell me the fee structure."

While they are telling it, cut in:

"I know the fee structure. It is too high."

Check · active listening & understanding

Asks what they know and what feels high vs tells the fee structure again

Understands the core concern vs focused on telling

Give feedback — if they repeat the fee or accept the no

"Don't explain the fee again. First ask: 'What makes it feel high — the amount, or whether it will really work for him?'

Then: learning skills is mandatory. If he doesn't learn now, later he goes to Hyderabad or Bengaluru for coaching, and PG rent alone is ₹8–10K a month. Even then he settles for a ₹20–25K job. Here, with about half of that rent, he learns the skills by final year and gets a good package. Venkateswarlu's father ran a small tea shop; they took the step, and he is placed at PayPal. So compared to the outcome, is it still high?"

Retake the mock
Check · coachability, across all retakes

Nothing new to ask. Coachability is the change between the first attempt and the retake in a and b. Did the retake change in the exact way you asked? Look at the change, not the quality.

Stage 4

Alignment & next steps

Ask · alignment

Work from office at <location> — fine? · Timings 11:30 AM – 9:30 PM? · Week-off on a weekday? · Own laptop? · Notice period?

A gate, not a score. A clear no on any of these ends it here.

If leaning select · tell them

₹15K stipend · ₹3.6 LPA after internship · 70 leads, 4 admissions expected · ₹10–20K incentive above 4 · hikes every 3–6 months · BDA → Sr BDA → TL → ABDM → BDM.

Everyone · tell them

Result in 7–10 days. Then ask "Any questions for me?" Their questions reveal their concerns.

Then update the candidate in the ATS tracker while it is fresh.

What we check now vs what we train later

Check now · takes a lot of time to train laterTrain later · do not test
Speaking with feel · Calm under rejection · Listening before telling · Applying feedback · Owning vs blaming · Seeking support · Conviction + needProduct details and numbers · Step 3 narrative · CTA wording · Lead prioritisation · VCs and closures · Follow-up discipline

Remember it like this: hire like a cricket opener

  • Rejection handling — doesn't flinch at the first bouncer.
  • Active listening — reads the bowler before swinging.
  • Coachability — takes the coach's tip into the very next over.
  • Ownership & seeking support — walks back after a duck and asks what went wrong.
  • Conviction + Need — plays for the team, not the scoreboard.
